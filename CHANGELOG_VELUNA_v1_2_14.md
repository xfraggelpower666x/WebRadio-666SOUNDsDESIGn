# VELUNA v1.2.14

- VELUNA mobile audio start race removed: no global first-touch capture, no blocking audio gate.
- PLAY is now the authoritative iPhone audio gesture.
- Stream start cycles through worker, fallback and direct reserve sources before failing.
- Normal iPhone player restored to its pre-theme geometry; central splash no longer mutates layout.
- Four mobile admin/message controls use a stable 2x2 grid.
- Oversized lower stream/admin/status labels are constrained for iPhone width.
