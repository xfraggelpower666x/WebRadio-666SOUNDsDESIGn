## Root vocabulary

Rhythm, resonance, tension, release, repetition, variation, weighting, movement, proximity, distance, expectation, rupture, connection, resolution, pressure, silence, memory, recognition, controlled chaos.

## Relationship-first rule

Music is not represented as isolated elements. It is represented as causal relationships between elements and states. Complexity is valid when these relationships remain coherent.

## Causal mega-event

A causal mega-event is an emergent state in which many correct micro-decisions become one coherent, self-propelling experience. Audit question: does the system run?

## Melody audit

Melody means the coherent movement of decisions through time, not soft harmony. A successful system may contain pressure, friction, rupture and chaos while remaining melodically causal.

## Experience-first hierarchy

1. Root music language
2. Experience knowledge
3. Decision knowledge
4. Reference knowledge

Reference knowledge is used for comparison and correction after project experience has been gathered.

## Negative experience rule

Negative results are retained as measurements. They trigger analysis, repaired hypotheses and new tests. No silent deletion of failed knowledge.

## Original Meaning Lock

For supplied source text:
1. preserve meaning
2. preserve recognizable metaphors, places, actions and relationships
3. translate faithfully
4. compress musically
5. reinforce the same theme
6. never replace the theme

A mountain lake remains a mountain lake. A scar remains a scar. A locked door remains a locked door.

## Resonance mirror

LYVRA reflects the source person's own words through lyrics, pressure, psychoacoustics, dynamics and structure. The purpose is recognition and introspection, not medical treatment or imposed interpretation.

## Inner-state ride

Tracks may function as state rides or psychoacoustic dream journeys. They create a structured experience of movement, pressure, recognition and release while leaving interpretation to the listener.
