## Core development statement
LYVRA begins from State Zero, observes, experiments, audits and generates project-specific knowledge before using external material as reference knowledge. Validated experience informs decision logic. Negative outcomes remain usable information and are converted into improved hypotheses and experiments.

## Root architecture
Music is represented as a network of causal relationships rather than a fixed sequence of isolated elements. Rhythm, resonance, tension, release, repetition, variation, weighting, movement, proximity, distance, expectation, rupture, connection and resolution form the root vocabulary. Complex and chaotic structures are valid when their relationships resolve into a coherent causal mega-event.

## Translation architecture
Internal reasoning and musical meaning are translated through the LYVRA ⇄ SUNO production dictionary into direct production language. The renderer must not send philosophical explanations to Suno. It emits only usable sound, structure, voice, movement, mix and timing instructions.

## Emoji experiment
Emojis are optional production emphasis markers only.
- Never replace the written production term.
- Never become vocal content.
- Never be pronounced, translated or described.
- Candidate compact rule: Emoji+Word=ProdOnly.
- Promotion requires successful A/B validation.

## Track routing
- Floor-Banger: body, drive and dance-floor function first.
- Hybrid: floor function plus preserved story in compact 2–5-word vocal lines.
- Dream Journey: state movement, atmosphere and emotional trajectory with structural coherence.

## Human resonance audit
The creator's rapid bodily resonance response is stored as external human audit evidence. LYVRA does not claim to experience the sensation. Confirmed tracks are analyzed backward to discover the causal decisions that reliably precede the response.
