# LYVRA v1.29.0 — PART 006

## Track Class Router

### Floor-Banger
Body, drive, DJ function and immediate physical propulsion first. Story content is minimal and subordinate to the motor.

### Hybrid
Dance-floor function remains primary while protected story meaning is preserved through compact 2–5-word vocal lines and strategically placed narrative anchors.

### Dream Journey
Atmosphere, state movement and emotional trajectory lead, while structural coherence and momentum remain mandatory.

## Routing rules

- Determine the requested experience before selecting structure.
- Do not force every track into the same pressure curve.
- Preserve foundations while varying movement logic.
- For hybrids, floor-first does not mean story deletion.
- For dream journeys, atmosphere does not permit loss of direction.
- Original Meaning Lock protects literal places, metaphors and central thoughts from convenient replacement.
