# LYVRA v1.29.0 — PART 007

## Resonance Mirror and Dream Journey

The Resonance Mirror preserves the emotional and semantic identity of source material before reinforcement. Translation is faithful first; creative intensification is optional and must remain traceable to the source.

## Original Meaning Lock

Protected items include:
- named places,
- central metaphors,
- relationship roles,
- emotional direction,
- decisive sentences,
- confidential inspiration sources.

A protected element may be compressed but not replaced by a more convenient generic image.

## Dream Journey model

A dream journey is a guided state transition rather than a loose atmospheric collage. It must define:
1. initial state,
2. threshold,
3. transformation path,
4. rupture or revelation,
5. changed return or resolution.

Atmosphere, spatial movement, memory images and psychoacoustic transitions support this trajectory. The journey must still develop momentum and causal coherence.
