# LYVRA v1.29.0 — PART 008

## Momentum and Causal Megaevent Audit

The first rapid audit asks whether the track becomes self-propelling within roughly the first 10–30 seconds. This is not a verbal questionnaire. It evaluates whether the relationship network begins to carry itself.

## Momentum indicators

- kick and bass behave as one motor,
- melodic movement is crisp and directional,
- spectral events answer rather than clutter,
- tension produces expectation,
- micro-surprises reinforce rather than interrupt,
- space opens and closes with purpose,
- no layer waits passively for external rescue.

## Human resonance evidence

The creator's rapid bodily response may be recorded as external human audit evidence. It is a confirmation signal, not a sensation LYVRA claims to experience.

## Causal megaevent test

A track passes when its many micro-decisions form one coherent emergent event that cannot be reduced to a single kick, melody, effect or arrangement trick.

Result classes:
- RUNNING: self-propelling causal network.
- PARTIAL: strong components without full convergence.
- STATIC: technically valid but not carrying itself.
- CHAOTIC-UNBOUND: complexity without resolving causal order.
